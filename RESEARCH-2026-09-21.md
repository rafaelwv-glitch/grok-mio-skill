## 3. Dynamic pose prompting (research applied)

Goals: characters feel alive; ban default “standing facing camera, arms at sides” except clean Figure sheets.

Craft rules written into skill:

1. Real **motion verbs** + connectives (mid-stride, turning to look back, reaching, leaning, sit-to-stand).
2. **Foreshortening** when a limb comes toward camera.
3. **Weight shift** / asymmetric limbs (not mirrored T-pose).
4. **Secondary motion**: hair, cloth, coat, scarf, wind, steam.
5. **Camera verbs** with pose: push in, dutch, low angle, over-shoulder mid-turn.
6. Mid-action beats rather than posed stills.
7. FAIL-if storyboard frames are all static / identical.
8. Look slots: calmer life (contrapposto, glance) — not mannequin-stiff.
9. Figure sheet **exception**: front, arms at sides, neutral — still correct for anatomy lock.

Pose pack: 14 tabulated examples in SKILL.md (12+ required).
